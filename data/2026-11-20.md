  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The RAR model, which models dark matter as degenerate massive fermions mimicking a central black hole plus a classical halo, has been constrained for the Milky Way with conflicting fermion-mass ranges (48–345 keV from earlier fits, 56 keV favored by S-star orbits, versus 194–387 keV from a recent thermodynamic stability analysis). Conclusion: Using the earlier Milky Way fermion-mass range, a single fermion mass of mc²=48–60 keV (including 56 keV) can describe both M31 and the Milky Way.
