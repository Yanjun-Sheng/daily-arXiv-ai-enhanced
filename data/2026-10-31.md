Method: An "anchor-and-refine" feedback scheme that anchors a host cell near each star particle to prevent displacement and preserve a thermal reservoir. These results highlight the role of thermal feedback in driving galactic winds. By preserving hot superbubble interiors and the partition of thermal and kinetic energy, anchor-and-refine improves the treatment of hot gas, star formation, and feedback at limited resolution, supporting future applications to galaxy-scale and cosmological zoom-in simulations.
