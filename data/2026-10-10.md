Infall velocity correlates moderately with non-thermal velocity dispersion and Mach number. GLASHES values exceed those of nearby low-mass star-forming cores but are below those of larger-scale high-mass clumps.

Conclusion: These results provide the first statistical constraints on core-scale mass accumulation during the earliest stages of high-mass star formation, indicating dynamical fast contraction and an evolution in the dominant physical process with core evolution.
