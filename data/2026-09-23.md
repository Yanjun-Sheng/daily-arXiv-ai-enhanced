*Maria Kopsacheili,Martín Guerrero,Diego Alejandro Vasquez Torres*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

Abstract: Novae are spectroscopically classified as Fe II or He/N according to the dominance of Fe II, or He and N emission lines in their pre-nebular spectra, while spectra exhibiting both are classified as hybrid.
